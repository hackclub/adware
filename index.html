What actually happens

Build and ship a project. Log the hours you spent coding it in Hackatime, then submit the project through the form below. I review the hours by hand and they land in your balance. You spend that balance on one of the three sticker sizes above and send me the artwork.

During the stream, when your order comes up, I print your sticker on an inkjet, cut it on a Cricut, and stick it to my laptop lid on camera. Then I post the sticker to you, batched in the week after the stream. Both things happen: it goes on the laptop, and you get one.

Every submission is approved before it prints. I can reject artwork and refund your hours. Nothing hateful, sexual, or targeting a person goes on my property or on a stream that minors watch.
